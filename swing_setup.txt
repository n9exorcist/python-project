THE TELL MOST PEOPLE MISS
Look at the two ends of that board. Electricals
+11.51%. Power −4.11%. Those are neighbours — and
they split hard.
Money is going into companies that make the equipment
— copper winding wire, cables, conductors — and out of
companies that generate electricity. That is a
transmission-and-distribution capex trade, not an energy
trade. It tells you which shelf to pull stocks from:
components, not utilities.
The one caution. Sector PE is 71.51 — expensive. You are buying momentum backed by earnings growth, not
value. That is a legitimate swing trade, but the downside on any disappointment is fast and deep. Size accordingly.

Most shapes on that sheet are one idea in different clothes: a contraction, then an expansion in the
direction of the prevailing trend. What you are really screening for is the contraction — a tight range on
falling volume, sitting above rising moving averages. The name you give the shape afterwards changes
nothing about the entry, the stop, or the size.
